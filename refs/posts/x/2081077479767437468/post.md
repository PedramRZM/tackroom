After spending ~24 hours working with Fable 5 and Opus 5 on a coding project these are my initial thoughts:

- Code quality for both is excellent
- Fable 5 does significantly better with open-ended prompts and requests and nails it the first time
- Opus 5 can do just as well, just needs a bit more direction
- Fable 5 spends more time understanding the code base and being thoughtful with implementation and pushing back when a better option exists
- Opus 5 seems to prefer to do what it's told even if a better route exists
- They're both really really good Claude's
