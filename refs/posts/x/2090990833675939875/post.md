The model is everything.

Ultimately, all ambition on the agent side is capped by the model’s long horizon reasoning and tool calling ability.

AI product work is therefore two things: claw back as much capability overhang as you can with in-context learning, and then put the model in situations it literally cannot handle.

The failures at that frontier are the data. Turn them into post-training. Shift capability left into the model.

The harness should be constantly working to make itself obsolete.
