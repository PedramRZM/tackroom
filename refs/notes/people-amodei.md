# Dario Amodei on AI and coding

Sources read: 2 essays (the passages on coding and software work in full, the rest skimmed by
search) and 7 X posts. Not obtained: his Code with Claude 2026 conversation with Daniela Amodei
(video `7xco5Qd2Oo8` has no English captions on any mirror), the essay announced on 2026-09-12
("We Must Pace the Frontier", not fetched), and long interviews (not fetched).

## Cross-cutting findings

1. He describes where coding is, not how to do it. Nothing in these sources is a practice,
   workflow or configuration; treat it as context for the other notes, not as evidence for a
   setup choice. (ADOL, intro and "Labor market disruption")
2. As of January 2026 he reports that some strong engineers, including at Anthropic, hand
   almost all code writing to AI. (ADOL)
3. He separates writing code from doing an engineer's job, and lists what remains. That list
   (testing, environments, deployment, product iteration) is the same ground the practice
   sources say needs harnessing: verification, sandboxes, CI, review. (ADOL footnote 38;
   the link to the practice sources is Inference.)
4. He expects the pace to increase because coding models speed up AI development itself. (ADOL)

Contradictions and open questions: none inside these sources. His claims are predictions and
first-hand impressions without measurements; the practice notes carry the numbers.

### The Adolescence of Technology (Dario Amodei, 2026-01-26)
File: [articles/darioamodei.com/essay-the-adolescence-of-technology/article.md](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology)
URL: https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology
Thesis: an essay on the risks of powerful AI and how to handle them. Coding appears as his
main example of how fast capability has moved.
Practices: none.
Evidence and numbers:
- Introduction: models are "good enough at coding that some of the strongest engineers I’ve
  ever met are now handing over almost all their coding to AI."
- "Labor market disruption", Speed: in two years models went from a single line to "writing
  all or almost all of the code" for some people, "including engineers at Anthropic." The link
  on that phrase points to a post by Boris Cherny.
- Same paragraph: "Soon, they may do the entire task of a software engineer end to end."
- Footnote 38 qualifies it: the two "are very different things, because software engineers do
  much more than just write code", and he names "testing, dealing with environments, files, and
  installation, managing cloud compute deployments, iterating on products".
- Same section, "Slicing by cognitive ability": in coding the models went from "a mediocre
  coder" to "a strong coder" to "a very strong coder."
Tools and files named: none.
Caveats: he flags uncertainty himself: "If the exponential continues—which is not certain".
Relevance: low for setup decisions; useful as the stated view of Anthropic's CEO on why the
bottleneck moves from writing code to everything around it.
