3. **Repeated failure and flakiness:** Stop repairs after the third unsuccessful attempt and escalate with each attempt, outputs and the smallest reproduction. Keep the fourth-run pass visible as **retry-green/flaky**, alongside the preceding failures; do not present it as a clean pass or keep rerunning unchanged code. Do not delete or weaken the test. Quarantine requires explicit owner approval, a repair task and an expiry, with the coverage gap reported. Current evidence cannot support an unqualified verified result.
